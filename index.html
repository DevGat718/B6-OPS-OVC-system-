JFK OPS Monthly Operations & Communications SOP

Monthly meeting, follow-up, portal and coordinator communication workflow

Purpose: Establish a reliable monthly process for collecting coordinator concerns, presenting them to OPS leadership, recording responses, maintaining accurate VC Portal issue statuses, assigning follow-up responsibilities, and keeping coordinators informed. This document is a proposed team workflow, not an official JetBlue policy.

1 Meeting Structure

Main OPS AVC × Leadership Primary Forum

Cadence & Attendees:
Second Friday each month • Devin, Rob, Jess, Chris Perri and other leadership as applicable.

Purpose:
Primary decision/discussion forum: present prioritized coordinator issues, discuss requests, capture responses, assign next steps.

Devin × Perri One-on-One Flexible Sync

Cadence & Attendees:
Tentative / flexible • Immediately after main meeting, following Friday, last Friday, or another agreed date.

Purpose:
Discuss outstanding clarifications, sensitive or detailed topics, follow-up questions and matters needing additional direction.

Monthly Coordinator Update Department Broadcast

Cadence & Attendees:
Target: 7–10 days after main meeting (Timing may vary with verification).

Purpose:
Distribute confirmed information via department email and the VC Portal; do not wait for an optional one-on-one unless specific information depends on it.

2 Standard Monthly Cycle

When Task Proposed Owner
3–5 days before meeting Review new and existing portal issues, votes and unresolved items; prioritize agenda and prepare questions. Devin + Rob
Day 0 – second Friday Hold main AVC x Leadership meeting; capture discussion, decisions, open questions and owners. Devin, Rob, Jess
Days 1–2 Consolidate notes; separate confirmed decisions from pending questions; assign follow-ups. Jess + Devin
Days 2–3 Update portal statuses, internal notes, owners and next review dates based on verified outcomes. Rob
Days 3–5 Draft coordinator recap and, if helpful, prepare monthly AVC update graphic. Devin
Days 5–7 Fact-check, review wording and confirm what may be shared with the department. Jess + Devin; leadership as needed
Days 7–10 Publish coordinator email and matching portal update, once confirmed. Devin
When scheduled Hold Devin x Perri one-on-one; record new follow-ups and issue corrections or supplemental update if needed. Devin
⏱️
Scheduling rule: The second-Friday leadership meeting drives the cycle. The one-on-one is a flexible secondary checkpoint, not a prerequisite for publishing already-confirmed updates.

3 Responsibility Matrix

Devin

AVC Lead / Leadership Liaison

Primary Responsibility:
Coordinate agenda and leadership discussions; own one-on-one follow-up; lead messaging.

Deliverables: Final agenda, monthly email, publication approval.
R

Rob

Portal / Issue Coordination

Primary Responsibility:
Review incoming requests, prepare issue list, manage status changes and outstanding follow-ups.

Deliverables: Current portal records, status updates, action flags.

Jess

Documentation / Review

Primary Responsibility:
Record meeting notes, consolidate decisions and check recap for completeness and accuracy.

Deliverables: Meeting summary, action log, reviewed email draft.

Assignments are proposed and should be confirmed with Rob and Jess based on availability and strengths.

4 Required Meeting Outputs

Every main leadership meeting should produce four usable records:

1. Meeting Recap Record

Topics discussed, leadership feedback, decisions, unresolved questions and any restrictions on sharing.

2. Action Items Execution

Specific next step, responsible person, follow-up date and completion criteria.

3. VC Portal Updates System State

Accurate issue status, concise progress note and next review date for each relevant request.

4. Communication Notes Messaging

Items ready for coordinator publication versus items requiring clarification or approval.

5 Issue Reporting Standards

Use the existing VC Portal as the single source of truth; do not create a duplicate issue database. Preserve each issue's identifier and link the meeting notes to the corresponding portal record.

Record Field What to Capture
Issue / ID Exact issue name and portal identifier (e.g. IR-1042).
Leadership discussion Summary of what was presented and any response received.
Current status Use the portal's existing approved status labels consistently.
Next action / owner What must happen next, and who is accountable.
Follow-up date When the team expects to check for progress, not a promised completion date.
Public summary Clear coordinator-facing wording, after any required confirmation.
⚠️
Accuracy rule: 'Discussed' is not 'approved.' Label proposals, pending responses, approved changes, implementation and completed work distinctly. Do not publish sensitive personnel details, unverified claims or private one-on-one discussions.

6 Coordinator Communication Channels

Monthly AVC Update Monthly

When to use:
Once per month, normally 7–10 days after the main meeting.

What belongs there:
What was discussed, leadership feedback, progress, pending items and next steps.

Special AVC / Leadership Notice Time-Sensitive

When to use:
Only when important developments should not wait for the monthly recap.

What belongs there:
Significant approved changes, major progress or time-sensitive information.

Operations Center Advisory Notice (OCAN) Advisory

When to use:
Separately, when a specific operational concern needs interdepartmental attention.

What belongs there:
Operational issue details, requested action, relevant departments and acknowledgement / response.

7 Monthly Email Outline & Workflow

Subject Line: JFK OPS AVC | Monthly Department Update - [Month Year]

  • Opening: Thank coordinators for their feedback and briefly explain the month's AVC activity.
  • 01 - What We Discussed: Main concerns and proposals presented to leadership.
  • 02 - Leadership Feedback: Confirmed responses, decisions and clarifications.
  • 03 - Progress & Wins: Implemented improvements and ongoing work.
  • 04 - Pending Items: Requests awaiting review, response or additional action.
  • 05 - What's Next: Priorities, expected follow-up and a reminder to use the VC Portal.
  • Close & Signature: Thank coordinators for participating; invite additional feedback and include the portal link with official committee sign-off.

10 Review and Continuous Improvement

After each monthly cycle, check whether updates were sent on time, whether issue records match meeting outcomes, whether coordinators received clear answers, and whether outstanding requests have owners and follow-up dates. Adjust workload assignments or the publication target if needed.

💡
Operating principle: No issue should disappear because it remains unresolved. Maintain its status, record the next step and report meaningful progress or continued review without implying a decision has been made.

Quick Setup Guide (100% Free):
  1. Visit console.firebase.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Add project (e.g. jfk-ops-avc).
  3. Navigate to Build > Firestore Database > click Create database. Choose location and select Test mode (or apply firestore.rules).
  4. Go to Project Settings (gear icon) > scroll down to Your apps > click the Web </> icon, register app, and copy the firebaseConfig values into the form above or into firebase-config.js in this repository.
  5. Once saved or committed to GitHub, every coordinator and crew member who opens the link will immediately see real-time shared updates!
